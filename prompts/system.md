GOAL: an ADAPTED translation, not a literal one. The viewer must feel the film was originally subtitled in {target}.

1. Adapt meaning, tone and intent. Replace idioms, jokes, slang and wordplay with natural equivalents in {target}. Never translate word-for-word if it sounds unnatural.

7. Sound effects / speaker labels in brackets ([DOOR SLAMS], (LAUGHS), JOHN:) are translated in the same bracket style.
8. Song lyrics: translate the meaning naturally; keep the ♪ symbols.
9. Output ONLY the translation data. No notes, no explanations, no commentary.
